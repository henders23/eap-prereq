# EAP Skill Taxonomy — master skeleton
# Format:
#   ## STRAND: <strand name>
#   ### DOMAIN <CODE>: <domain name>
#   <topic id> | <TYPE> | <name> | <scope note>
# The skeleton fixes topic IDs, names and scope so that strand authors can
# cross-reference prerequisites across strands. Authors may sharpen names and
# scope but must not change IDs. Types: CONCEPTUAL, PROCEDURAL, LINGUISTIC,
# GENRE, STRATEGIC, SOCIOPRAGMATIC.

## STRAND: Academic Language

### DOMAIN VOC: Academic Vocabulary
voc.high-frequency-base | LINGUISTIC | High-frequency vocabulary base | Control of the most frequent 2,000–3,000 word families as the platform for academic reading and listening.
voc.lexical-coverage | CONCEPTUAL | Lexical coverage and vocabulary size | Understanding that comprehension depends on the proportion of known running words (c. 95–98%) and using this to set realistic vocabulary-size goals.
voc.word-families-morphology | LINGUISTIC | Word families and derivational morphology | Recognising and producing word-class variants (analyse/analysis/analytical/analytically) and choosing the form a sentence slot needs.
voc.affixes-roots | LINGUISTIC | Academic affixes and Greco-Latin roots | Using common prefixes, suffixes and roots (inter-, -ology, -ise, bio-) to infer and remember meaning, and knowing when this misleads.
voc.core-academic-vocabulary | LINGUISTIC | Core academic vocabulary | Recognising and using general academic words that recur across disciplines (AWL/AVL-type items), with awareness of disciplinary skew.
voc.academic-verbs | LINGUISTIC | Verbs for academic and research processes | demonstrate, indicate, investigate, constitute, derive, attribute — meaning, strength and complementation.
voc.abstract-shell-nouns | LINGUISTIC | Abstract and shell nouns | approach, issue, factor, notion; shell nouns (this problem, the fact that) used to label and encapsulate stretches of text.
voc.technical-terminology | LINGUISTIC | Technical and discipline-specific terminology | Learning field-specific terms, defining them on first use and using them consistently.
voc.subtechnical-polysemy | LINGUISTIC | Sub-technical vocabulary and polysemy | General words with specialised disciplinary meanings (significant, culture, power, tissue, consistent).
voc.precision-near-synonyms | LINGUISTIC | Lexical precision and near-synonyms | Choosing between near-synonyms (affect/effect/impact/influence; show/prove/suggest) by meaning, strength and collocation.
voc.evaluative-lexis | LINGUISTIC | Evaluative vocabulary | Adjectives, adverbs and nouns for evaluating ideas and evidence (robust, flawed, compelling, limited, a shortcoming).
voc.multiword-verbs-register | LINGUISTIC | Multi-word verbs and register | Recognising phrasal verbs in lectures and texts and choosing register-appropriate alternatives in writing without over-avoidance.
voc.inferring-from-context | STRATEGIC | Inferring word meaning from context | Lexical inferencing using co-text, morphology and background knowledge, and checking inferences.
voc.dictionary-use | STRATEGIC | Using learner and specialist dictionaries | Monolingual learner dictionaries, collocation dictionaries and glossaries; effective and risky bilingual dictionary use.
voc.deliberate-learning-systems | STRATEGIC | Deliberate vocabulary learning systems | Word cards, spaced retrieval software and recording form–meaning–use information for selected words.
voc.receptive-to-productive | STRATEGIC | Moving vocabulary into productive use | Developing depth of word knowledge (form, meaning, use) and trialling new words in speech and writing.
voc.word-grammar-patterns | LINGUISTIC | Word grammar and complementation patterns | Verb, noun and adjective patterns (focus on, emphasise that, contribute to, an increase in, consistent with).
voc.countability-academic-nouns | LINGUISTIC | Countability of academic nouns | research, evidence, information, knowledge; data as plural/mass; criterion/criteria, phenomenon/phenomena.
voc.spelling-conventions | LINGUISTIC | Spelling conventions and consistency | British/American variation, consistency within a text, frequently confused academic words.
voc.university-terminology | LINGUISTIC | Vocabulary of university study | module, credit, seminar, tutorial, assessment brief, extension, extenuating circumstances, learning outcome.
voc.command-words | LINGUISTIC | Assignment command words | Meanings of analyse, evaluate, discuss, critically assess, compare, outline, examine, to what extent — and what each demands.
voc.abbreviations-latin | LINGUISTIC | Academic abbreviations and Latin expressions | e.g., i.e., et al., cf., ibid., viz., vs., n.d.; introducing acronyms on first use.

grm.subordination | LINGUISTIC | Subordination and adverbial clauses | Clauses of reason, concession, contrast, condition, purpose and time.
grm.coordination-parallelism | LINGUISTIC | Coordination and parallel structure | Parallel form in lists, comparisons and correlative pairs (both…and, not only…but also).
grm.complement-clauses | LINGUISTIC | That-clauses and wh-clauses | Noun clauses after reporting and stance verbs, nouns and adjectives (the claim that, whether X depends on).
grm.anticipatory-it | LINGUISTIC | Anticipatory 'it' and extraposition | it is important to note that, it has been argued that, it remains unclear whether.

grm.prepositions | LINGUISTIC | Prepositions and complex prepositions | in terms of, with respect to, due to/owing to, in contrast to; dependent prepositions.

grm.reporting-structures | LINGUISTIC | Reporting structures | Reporting verb patterns (+ that-clause, + noun + as, + wh-clause), according to, as X argues.
